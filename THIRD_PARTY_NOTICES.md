## Scenery

RCForge bundles CC0 surface textures and a sky HDRI from Poly Haven and ambientCG. See [the asset manifest](public/scenery/README.md) for each source, creator and license. The foliage atlas was generated specifically for RCForge; its original alpha channel and generation prompt are retained. Alpine and mesa terrain also bundle derived Mapzen Terrain Tiles elevation data. Europe terrain data: produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union – EU-DEM layers. United States 3DEP (formerly NED) and global GMTED2010 and SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. See the asset manifest for source URLs, modifications and data licensing. These asset credits are separate from the MIT license for RCForge's code.
